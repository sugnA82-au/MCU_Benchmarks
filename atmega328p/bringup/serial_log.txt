

=============================================
  ATmega328P BRING-UP
=============================================
  compiler      : GCC 7.3.0
  MCUSR (reset) : 0x00  (cleared by Optiboot before app start)

  -- fuses, read from the running chip --
  low fuse      : 0xff  CKSEL=f SUT=3 CKDIV8=1
  high fuse     : 0xde
  ext fuse      : 0xfd
  lock bits     : 0xcf
  -> clock src  : external crystal 8 MHz+, CKDIV8 off (full speed)

  -- Timer1 vs the watchdog's own RC oscillator --
  131072 WDT clk: 2420679 Timer1 ticks
  -> WDT RC at   108293 Hz, if the system clock is 16 MHz
     (would be   54146 Hz at 8 MHz -- not a rate this RC runs)
  [ OK ] system clock is the 16 MHz crystal, not the 8 MHz RC

  -- GPIO --
  DDRB          : 0x20  (PB5 output, on-board LED)
  D2 pulled up  : 1  (expect 1)
  D2 driven low : 0  (expect 0)
  D2 released   : 1  (expect 1)
  [ OK ] GPIO input path reads both levels

  -- blinking at 1 Hz, one line per 5 s --
  Compare the us column against wall-clock time on the host:
  that is the only check here that does not trust the chip.

  step  10  elapsed 5000007 us  (expect 5000000)
  step  20  elapsed 10000007 us  (expect 10000000)
